Rancho Agua Puerca Y Las Trancas (1843)

Granted 1843 by Gov. Micheltorena to R. Rodriguez and F. Alviso. Present-day Santa Cruz County. 4,421 acres. U.S. land case ND 250: rejected by the Board of Land Commissioners, then confirmed on appeal. Patented to Ramon Rodriguez and Francisco Alviso Mar. 1, 1867, 4,421.52 acres.
