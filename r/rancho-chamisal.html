Rancho El Chamisal (1835)

Granted 1835 by Gov. Jose Castro to F. Vasquez. Present-day Monterey County. 2,737 acres. U.S. land case SD 222: rejected by the Board of Land Commissioners, then confirmed on appeal. Patented to the heirs of Felipe Vasquez Mar. 20, 1877, 2,737.44 acres.
