Rancho San Antonio (Lugo) (1838)

Granted 1810 by Gov. Jose Dario de Arguello (finally Alvarado) to A. M. Lugo. Present-day Los Angeles County. 29,513 acres. U.S. land case SD 009: Confirmed.
