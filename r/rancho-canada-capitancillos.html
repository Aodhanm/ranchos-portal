Rancho Canada De Los Capitancillos (1842)

Granted 1842 by Gov. Alvarado to Justo Larios. Present-day Santa Clara County. 3,360 acres. U.S. land case ND 132: confirmed by the Board of Land Commissioners. Patented to Charles Fossatt Feb. 3, 1865, 3,360.48 acres.
