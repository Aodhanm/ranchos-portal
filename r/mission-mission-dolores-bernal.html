Mission Dolores (Bernal) (1834)

Ex-mission lands (Mission Dolores (Bernal)), secularized and later adjudicated as a U.S. land claim. Claimant: Carmen Sibrian de Bernal and José de Jesús Bernal. U.S. land case ND 165.
