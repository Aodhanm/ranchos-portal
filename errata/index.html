How corrections are found

Two ways. A blind sample of 100 of the 672 records was drawn on 1 September 2026, committed to the public repository with its checksum before any of it was checked, and was then verified field by field against the land case files; that pass is complete and its results are in section 10 of the Sources and Method page. And readers write in. If a grant here contradicts a document you hold, the document wins: aodhancoyne@gmail.com.

What counts as an erratum

A value that was published and is wrong. A field left blank because no source states it is not an error, and is not listed here. Where the register and a source disagree about a grant, the land case file is the authority.
