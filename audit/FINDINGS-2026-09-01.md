# Blind accuracy audit of the ranchos register — results

**Sample:** 100 of 672 records, drawn by fixed public seed and hash-committed on
2026-09-01 before any checking (see `README.md`). Every record verified field by
field against its Bancroft land case file PDF (the primary source), plus a
deterministic cross-check of all 672 records against Ogden Hoffman's 1862 printed
*Table of Land Claims*. Verification complete: **100 of 100**.

Grades: **OK** register agrees with the source · **ERR** register wrong ·
**PART** right entity, imprecise form · **UV** not determinable from the pages
read · **NA** field deliberately blank. Blank-by-design is compliance, not error,
and UV is reported separately, never counted as correct.

## Headline

- **49 of 100 records are exactly right on every gradable field.**
- **25 of 100 records carry at least one confirmed error.**
- Overall field error rate: **30 of 568 checkable field-grades = 5.3%.**

## The systematic finding: claimant recorded as original grantee

The `grantee` field is the register's weak point, and the failure is not random.
In the large majority of the 16 grantee errors, the field carries the name of the
**U.S.-era land case claimant** — an American purchaser, or a Californio heir who
filed the 1850s confirmation petition — instead of the **original Mexican grantee**
named in the concession decree. Confirmed instances:

- **Purchasers recorded as grantee:** Soulajule (José Ramón Mesa was grantee; the
  register lists the buyers Brackett / Vásquez / Gormley across its three rows),
  Rancho del Puerto (Hernández brothers, not the Boston claimants Reid/Wade),
  Nicasio (de la Guerra + Cooper, not Halleck/Black/Buckelew), Quito
  (Fernández/Noriega, not Alviso), Vega del Río Pájaro (Antonio María Castro, not
  the purchaser Anzar), Zanjones (Gabriel de la Torre, not the executor Malarín),
  Las Posas (José Carrillo, not de la Guerra).
- **Heirs recorded as grantee:** Las Cruces (Miguel Cordero, not his son Vicente),
  Cuyama (Cesario Lataillade, not his widow), San Antonio (Prado Mesa, not the heir
  Encarnación), Rincón de los Esteros (Ygnacio Alviso, not Rafael).
- **Plain misreadings:** Cosumnes ("Salvador Oslo" corresponds to nothing; grantee
  was W. E. P. Hartnell), Laguna de la Merced (José Antonio Galindo, not Josefa de
  Haro), San Luis Gonzaga (Mejía + Pacheco).

This is one identifiable, fixable class of error, and it very likely repeats across
the full register — the census pass (Phase B) targets it.

## The most serious single error: a rejected claim shown as confirmed

- **u-23, Orchard of San Juan Bautista (SD 385):** register outcome "Confirmed."
  The case file shows the Land Commission confirmed (18 Dec 1855) but the **District
  Court reversed and REJECTED** the claim on 6 March 1862 (Judge Fletcher M. Haight),
  holding Pico's 1846 sale of mission-orchard land to pay wartime debts an
  unauthorized transfer. This is the one field where a wrong value asserts good
  title that does not exist. Every other "Confirmed" in the sample was traced
  through the District and Supreme Court decrees and held up — including several
  where the Land Commission had first rejected and a higher court reversed
  (Cucamonga, Cañada de Raymundo, Peñasquitos, Cuyama), the opposite trap.

## What the audit did NOT find

No errors in `land_case` (the docket scaffolding is sound) and no errors in `name`.
Governor errors (4) are mostly the interim-governor problem: grants signed by Manuel
Jimeno as acting governor are attributed to Alvarado (San Jacinto Viejo, Zanjones).
Year errors (9) are single-value slips, often a provisional-grant date standing in
for the formal title, or vice versa (Atascadero, Peñasquitos, San Pascual, Mallacomes).

## Patent fields (Phase C)

No clean public-domain full-text source provides the patent ISSUE DATE or the GLO
patent NUMBER (the Surveyor-General reports cover only agricultural and swamp/school
land; the one Spanish-grant patent table has unusable OCR; the BLM API was ruled out
on policy — it required an extracted credential). Hoffman 1862 supplies patentee
name and acreage. Plan: grade `patent_to` against Hoffman where stated, and grade
`patent_date` and `glo_patent_no` as UV. This is honest under the register's own
rule that an unverifiable value is reported as such.
