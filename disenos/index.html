Diseños

1870 manuscript diseños, the hand-drawn maps filed with Spanish and Mexican land-grant claims, arranged by county across 38 groups. Held by The Bancroft Library.

Manuscript diseños (Mexican land-grant maps) from the U.S. private land-claim cases, held by The Bancroft Library, UC Berkeley. Every pin links to the archival original. County placement is from the catalog title or a strict match to the state's land-grant dataset; ambiguous names are left unplaced. Labels are working descriptions; scholarly headnotes are being added.
