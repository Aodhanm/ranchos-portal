The Pico family

Patriarch: José María Pico (1764–1819); the California founder was his father Santiago de la Cruz Pico, an Anza colonist of 1775–76

From a soldier's family of modest and mixed origins the Picos rose to the summit of Mexican California: Pío Pico twice governed the territory and, with his brother Andrés, held some of the largest southern ranchos. The dynasty's prominence was political and military as much as landed.

Origin

Santiago de la Cruz Pico came overland on the Anza expedition; his son José María Pico served as a soldier at San Diego and Mission San Luis Rey. The family was of mixed (partly African and Indian) ancestry, typical of the frontier soldiery.
