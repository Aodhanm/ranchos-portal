The Sánchez family

Patriarch: Two lines: (1) José Antonio Sánchez (1773; Anza-colonist child, San Francisco presidio soldier); (2) a separate Los Angeles Sánchez family (Vicente Sánchez)

The register's Sánchez grants belong to at least two unrelated families: the northern (peninsula) Sánchezes descended from Anza-colonist José Antonio, holders of Rancho Buri Buri and Rancho San Pedro, and a Los Angeles Sánchez family (Vicente Sánchez, Tomás Sánchez). The common surname makes surname-level grouping unreliable.

Origin

José Antonio Sánchez came north as a child with the Anza expedition and served long at the San Francisco presidio, receiving Rancho Buri Buri. A distinct Sánchez family was prominent in Los Angeles.
