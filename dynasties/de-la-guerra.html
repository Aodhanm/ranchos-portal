The de la Guerra family

Patriarch: José de la Guerra y Noriega (1779 Novales, Santander, Spain – 1858 Santa Barbara)

The de la Guerras were the wealthiest and most socially prominent family of Santa Barbara, controlling a near-continuous belt of ranchos from San Luis Obispo to Ventura counties. Their power rested on the presidial command, a merchant fortune, and marriage into the Carrillo family.

Origin

Unlike the soldier-founders, de la Guerra came from Spain via a merchant uncle in Mexico City and entered the army as an officer, becoming comandante of the Santa Barbara presidio (1815–1842); he married Antonia Carrillo, daughter of José Raimundo Carrillo.
