The Vallejo family

Patriarch: Ignacio Vicente Ferrer Vallejo (1748 Jalisco – 1832 Monterey)

The Vallejos rose from a presidial soldier's family to the dominant land-holding and military dynasty of the northern frontier. Mariano G. Vallejo's command at Sonoma let him and his brother Salvador assemble vast Napa–Sonoma–Clear Lake holdings in the 1830s–40s, making them the pre-eminent Californio family north of the bay.

Origin

Ignacio Vallejo enlisted in the colonial army in Guadalajara and came to Alta California in 1774 as a soldier attached to the Franciscan missionary guard; he served ~50 years, rising to distinguished sergeant, and married María Antonia Lugo (of the Lugo family).
